## 内容提要 / Abstract

全书分三部分：框架、经营分析与估值的工具、经营分析与估值的应用。帮助读者分析任一企业的财务报表，揭示真实经营情况与价值，并将战略经营知识整合进财务分析。
Three parts: framework, tools, and applications — enables analysis of any company's financial statements to reveal its true operations and value, integrating strategic business knowledge into financial analysis.
